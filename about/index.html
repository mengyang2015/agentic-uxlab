About the Lab

Better agents begin with better human experiences.

What we're exploring

The Agentic UX Lab explores how people prepare, use, supervise, and improve AI agents.

The goal is simple: turn emerging research and practical lessons into clear frameworks and design patterns that help people work confidently with autonomous systems.

Three areas of focus.

Frameworks

Models for designing the complete agent experience.

Patterns

Useful approaches for trust, control, and recovery.

Research

Insights grounded in public evidence and real product behavior.

Behind the Lab
